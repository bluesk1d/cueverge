Your display. Your setup.

Creative on the surface.
Considered underneath.

One show, coordinated outputs

The controller chooses the scene. Lighting follows authoritative media time, or an explicit controller clock for LED-only scenes.

Run it on your own terms

A self-hosted Python engine, a browser workspace, Windows and Linux media agents, and opt-in RGB DDP and Frigate MQTT adapters.

Room to experiment

Hardware-free examples and deterministic simulations give you a place to start. Live output stays a deliberate next step.
